Contact

For help, feedback or bug reports, email takii.hor@gmail.com. The app is operated by Lung Wai Hor.

Please include your app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. You may attach a screenshot if helpful. Do not send passwords or unrelated personal information.

Local game data

The app runs offline without an online account. Game saves, history and preferences are stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove this data. The app does not provide a developer-operated cloud backup service.
