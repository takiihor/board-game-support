Volt Odyssey — Support

Updated / 更新日: 2026-10-05

Offline journeys

Volt Odyssey is an offline 3D game with country journeys across China, Japan, Greece, Egypt and Iceland. It needs no account or network connection.

Controls and settings

Use the on-screen controls to steer, jump, dash and pause. Settings provides English and Japanese UI, graphics quality, touch-control preferences and audio options.

Progress

Pause and results screens provide a return to the main menu. Returning home preserves progress at checkpoint boundaries rather than the exact position of the character. Updates preserve local progress; deleting the app may remove it.

Rendering or heat

Choose a lower graphics quality in Settings if needed. Device performance varies; no frame-rate guarantee is offered.

Report a problem

Use the public GitHub support link below. Include your device model, iOS version, app version/build, country or stage, reproduction steps, expected result and actual result. A game screenshot can help. Do not include personal information, passwords or unrelated private files.
