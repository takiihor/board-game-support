Volt Odyssey — Privacy policy

Updated / 更新日: 2026-10-05

Offline operation

Volt Odyssey requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics service, tracking SDK, purchases or developer-operated server connection. The app does not collect or send personal information to the developer.

Local data

Progress, checkpoints, settings, language preferences and in-game postcards are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. Postcards are game images. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo library.

Managing data

You can reset game progress in Settings. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to device backup and restore behavior controlled by your operating system.

Apple services

Apple may provide device backup or optional operating-system diagnostics under its own settings and privacy policy. Those services are separate from this game’s code.

Optional support reports

If you choose to submit a public support issue, GitHub processes that information under its own privacy policy. A support report may contain the information you choose to provide. Avoid including private information or personal files.
