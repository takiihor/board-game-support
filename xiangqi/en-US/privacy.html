Xiangqi: Chinese Chess — Privacy policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Operator and contact

Lung Wai Hor · takii.hor@gmail.com

Offline play and local storage

The app is designed for offline board-game play. Computer opponents run on your device. No account or sign-in is required. The app does not send your moves, saved games or preferences to a developer server. Game history, progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising or developer analytics service. It does not track users across other apps or websites or collect personal data through gameplay.

Deleting local data

You can remove local app data by deleting the app. There is no developer-operated cloud copy of those game saves to delete.

Support requests and platform services

If you email support, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Email providers process those messages under their own policies. Apple may process App Store, device or TestFlight information under Apple’s privacy policy.

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Requests may be processed under GitHub’s privacy statement. This website uses no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies.
