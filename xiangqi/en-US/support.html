Contact

For help, feedback or bug reports, email takii.hor@gmail.com. The app is operated by Lung Wai Hor.

Please include your app version, device model, operating-system version and the steps that caused the problem. You may attach a helpful screenshot. Do not send passwords or unrelated personal information.

Offline play

Take turns with a friend on the same device or play against the on-device computer. Choose English in the app for English menus, guided rules, endgame practice and position study. Chess pieces retain their traditional Chinese symbols.

Local game data

The app runs offline without an online account. Game saves, history and preferences are stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove this data. The app does not provide a developer-operated cloud backup service.
