Insects: ExploreWorld — Support

Insects: ExploreWorld is an offline natural-history collection for iPhone and iPad. Its 26 interactive, scanned specimens, field notes and attribution are included in the app. No account is required.

Explore the collection

Open Collection, search for a specimen and open its record. Drag to rotate, use the viewpoint and zoom controls, or choose Focus on specimen for the observation view. Anatomy lets you inspect mapped external structures where available. The heart button saves a specimen to the notebook on your device. You can switch between English and Traditional Chinese.

These are surface scans of preserved specimens, shown at magnified scale. They are not living insects and do not establish measured biological scale, molecular identification or internal anatomy. Known reconstruction and identification limits, specimen sources and licences are recorded in the app.

Get help

For a problem, feedback or a feature request, email takii.hor@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, specimen name and steps to reproduce the issue. Please do not send passwords or unrelated personal information.
