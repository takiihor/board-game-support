Insects: ExploreWorld — Privacy policy

Updated 10 October 2026. This policy describes the Insects: ExploreWorld iPhone and iPad app.

Information on your device

The app stores saved-specimen IDs, recent and last-viewed specimens, language, label visibility and display-quality preferences locally. It has no sign-in, developer-operated cloud sync, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

The bundled collection runs offline. The app does not upload your notebook or preferences to the developer. Use heart buttons to remove saved records and the app settings to change display preferences. Deleting the app removes its local container; device backups and restores are controlled by your Apple/iOS settings.

External links, TestFlight and support

External specimen-source links may open a browser and are subject to those websites’ privacy practices. Apple provides TestFlight distribution and may handle installation, usage, crash and feedback information under its own terms. If you choose to send feedback through TestFlight or email support, the developer receives the information you choose to share and uses it to investigate and respond to the issue. Avoid unnecessary personal information.

These public support and privacy pages have no advertising, tracking scripts or contact form. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process ordinary web-request information under its privacy statement.
