ExploreWorld Human Body — Privacy policy

Updated 10 October 2026. This policy describes the ExploreWorld Human Body iPhone and iPad app.

Information on your device

The app stores your chosen language and explanation-depth preference locally. Models and educational content are bundled for offline use. No account, developer-operated backend, advertising or analytics/tracking SDK is used. The app does not upload these preferences or model interactions to the developer.

External links, TestFlight and support

Source links can open other websites, which have their own privacy policies. Apple provides TestFlight distribution and may handle installation, crash and feedback information under its own terms. If you choose to send TestFlight feedback or email support, the developer receives the information you choose to share and uses it to investigate and respond. Please avoid sending medical records or unnecessary personal details.

These public support and privacy pages use no advertising, tracking scripts or contact form. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process ordinary web-request information under its privacy statement.
