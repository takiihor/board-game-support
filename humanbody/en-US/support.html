ExploreWorld Human Body — Support

ExploreWorld Human Body is an educational app for exploring anatomical models, organs, senses and cells. The iPhone and iPad app includes its learning content and models for offline use. No account is required.

Get help

For a problem, general feedback or a feature request, email takii.hor@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, selected language and the steps to reproduce the issue. For a model-view problem, include the study name and the selected structure.

The app is a learning aid. Models and authored schematics simplify shapes, scales and colours. Sources, credits and model limitations are available in the app. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment guidance or patient-specific medical advice.
